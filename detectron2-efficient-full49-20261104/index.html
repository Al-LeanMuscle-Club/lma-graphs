What these checks do not establish

  • These are observations for the declared tests and configurations, not proof of full functional equivalence.
  • The 160 CPU results count command groups; the 38 supplemental scenarios and 23 GPU nodes have different denominators and must not be added into one feature-pass percentage.
  • Graph containers and related checks are context only. Passing a related child or shared check does not independently verify the parent capability.
  • The representative modification workflow includes executing a small change; it does not separately measure understanding, diagnosis or general execution difficulty.
  • Token improvement requires the frozen development and held-out rules. An unrun, failed, resource-blocked or inconclusive result is never a pass.
  • The pretrained comparison covers one Mask R-CNN R50-FPN checkpoint and one COCO image, not dataset AP or broad checkpoint compatibility.
  • Training convergence, multi-rank training, broad AMP/GPU coverage and optional integration paths remain outside the demonstrated evidence.
  • The known 39 historical unresolved test IDs remain evidence gaps; historical failures, errors, skips and timeouts have not all been demonstrated to be resource-only blockers.
  • There are 12 overlapping CPU/GPU upstream test IDs: 319 + 23 yields 330 unique IDs, not 342 unique tests. This is not an exhaustive feature denominator.
  • Physical lines are only a size measure. They do not establish readability or general cognitive cost.
  • KNOWLEDGE_GAPS.md describes what the author knew and self-tested before external validation. Independent CPU, GPU and pretrained observations are reported separately here; the 92 unknown understanding contracts and historical coverage gaps are not silently resolved.
