FROM OVERVIEW TO IMPLEMENTATION

A tree that grows with your questions.

  1. Open one branch at a time

    Click a domain to reveal its modules. Click a module to reveal its steps. Other open branches stay visible. Click again to collapse.

  2. Choose what to keep

    Use the checkboxes independently of expansion. A domain applies to its modules; functional steps follow their owning module. Required core and dependencies stay protected.

  3. Review and submit

    Review your retained modules, set the source token target, and submit your selection. The target controls the rewrite budget, not the number of selected modules.
